EcoRpg v0.2
(Economic, not ecologic)

Goal:
Defeat dragon, get rich.

Shortcuts:
1-2 - manage ally
C - character screen
D - go down
E - explore
F - forage
G - guild
H - house
J - journal
P - properties (city)
P - prospect (cave)
R - rest
T - travel
U - go up
S - shop
W - work
X - exit or enter

Combat:
Left button - focus on enemy

Shop/Craft:
Left button - buy/sell/craft one
Left Ctrl + Left button - pick how much to buy/sell/craft
Left Shift + Left button - sell/craft whole stack

Guild:
R - recruit
C - craft
K - cook

Map:
Left button - travel
Left Shift + Left button - travel without entering location
Right button - move map
Mouse wheel - zoom in/out map

Skills:
Alchemy - create extra potions and better recipes
Management - ???
Mining - more resources when mining, extra payment in mine
Woodwork - extra payment in sawmill
