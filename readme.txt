EcoRpg v0.1
(Economic, not ecologic)

Goal:
Defeat dragon, get rich.

Shortcuts:
1-2 - manage ally
C - character screen
E - explore
F - forage
G - guild
H - house
P - properties
R - rest
T - travel
S - shop
W - work
X - exit or enter

Shop:
Click - buy/sell one
Left Ctrl + click - pick how much to buy/sell
Left Shift + click - sell whole stack

Guild:
R - recruit
C - craft
F - finish quest

Map:
Click - travel
Left Shift + click - travel without entering location

Skills:
Alchemy - create extra potions
Mining - extra payment in mine
Woodwork - extra payment in sawmill
